Aravax, a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing next-generation, disease-modifying immunotherapies for food allergy, today announces it will be communicating topline results from its Phase 2 study of its lead product, PVX108 for the treatment of peanut allergy, at major conferences in the US and Europe in November 2026.
